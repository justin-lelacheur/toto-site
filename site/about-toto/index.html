About toto

Enterprise software should stay close to the enterprise it serves.

toto is the company building toto foundry, an AI-native enterprise operating system for organizations that need to change their operations without giving up clarity, control, or continuity.

A product with a point of view

Technology should express business intent, not obscure it.

toto builds around a simple conviction: people who are accountable for the business should be able to see, shape, and govern the software that carries the business forward. toto foundry turns that conviction into shared semantics, durable processes, clear ownership, and AI that works within explicit boundaries.

How toto foundry is built

Four principles guide the platform.

Meaning before interfaces

Business entities, relationships, policy, and lifecycle are shared before screens, integrations, and automations are designed.

Authority before automation

Agents can help with the work, but owned commands, policy checks, and people remain in charge of material effects.

Evolution before replacement

Existing systems can be modernized in stages through clear contracts, migration evidence, and reversible change.

Evidence before claims

Every production capability needs accountable ownership, observability, recovery, security, and a way to prove it is ready.

Open where it matters

Built to connect. Designed to remain governable.

toto foundry uses open standards and proven open-source foundations where they provide durable value. Components remain replaceable, but the business model, contracts, controls, and tenant boundaries stay explicit.

The first toto foundry module

Prove the model where relationships matter most.

CRM is the first complete toto foundry domain module. It establishes the customer data, identity, workflow, agent, and support foundations that future domains can reuse with confidence.
